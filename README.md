### 🏗️ The Grounded Truth (Mission)

I build systems that must survive contact with reality: **POC before narrative, receipts before claims, and resilient local operation before cloud dependence**.

At **Kopano Labs**, I work across AI orchestration, governance, adaptive PWAs, edge systems, robotics, data infrastructure, and open-source protocols. The objective is not more software for its own sake; it is technology that reduces real friction for people, especially where bandwidth, power, cost, access, and unemployment change what "working" actually means.

My operating rule is simple: **if a concept cannot be evidenced, tested, observed, or honestly left unresolved, it is not ready to be treated as reality.**

> **Foundation:** *Jesus is King and died for our sins so we may receive salvation. Every line of code, every protocol, and every system I architect is built on integrity, excellence, and service.*
