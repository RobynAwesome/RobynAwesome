# AUGUST 2026 — ESTATE EXECUTION DIRECTIVE

This directive applies across active RobynAwesome / Kopano Labs product repositories for August 2026.

## Monthly target

`WORKING → CONNECTED → CURRENT → VISIBLE → EVIDENCED → BACKABLE`

The objective is to harden and expose the MVP estate before the Kopano Labs AI product is deployed in the next 6–12 months.

## Public product rule

Show what is happening now. Prefer product, video, image, screenshot, 3D/spatial object and state data before explanatory prose.

## FOC validation

Every active MVP separates: WORKS / VISIBLE / BACKING / CURRENT / CONNECTED.

Owner-validated FOC groups currently implemented:

- Fake of Concept — presentation outruns evidence/state.
- Freedom of Concept — agency exists but remains governed by consequence, evidence and ownership.

Do not invent additional groups without an owner definition.

## Toolchain

TypeScript repositories target **TypeScript 7**. Do not downgrade to TypeScript 5/6 for convenience. Repair compatibility against TS7 and keep dependencies/libraries current with build/runtime receipts.

## Block rule

Before stopping delivery: explain what failed, why it matters, whether it is still required, what can continue, and a materially different workaround. Retract obsolete requirements rather than repeatedly repairing them.

## Backing focus

Products need the right eyes, data and backing—not merely existence. Public surfaces should make current state, proof, gaps and backing targets easy to inspect.
